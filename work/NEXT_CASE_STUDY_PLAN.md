## How To Add It

Use the Week 2 three-beat structure:

1. **Problem:** State the operational question and why it matters. Keep it to two or three sentences with the decision-maker and success measure named.
2. **What I did:** Describe the data preparation, baseline, model or analysis, validation approach, and the one or two plots that make the method understandable.
3. **What came of it:** Report the measured result, explain the practical recommendation, and name one limitation or next test.

Before publishing, add the notebook, reproducible outputs, one concise case-study page, and a link from the portfolio. Keep data anonymized and frame results as measured decision support.

## Named Next Piece

**SEO Refresh Monitoring: Measuring Whether the Priority Queue Finds Real Content Decay**

The project will compare the existing refresh-priority queue with later page outcomes. It will define a post-refresh evaluation window, measure changes in impressions, clicks, CTR, and position, and report whether high-priority recommendations show stronger observed recovery than lower-priority pages. The analysis will distinguish association from causal impact and document the limitation if no controlled refresh experiment is available.
